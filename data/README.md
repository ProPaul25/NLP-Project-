# Datasets

## Chauhan et al. Kangri corpus — `raw/chauhan_kangri/`
- Source: https://github.com/chauhanshweta/Kangri_corpus
- Paper: Chauhan, Saxena, Daniel (2021). Monolingual and Parallel Corpora for Kangri Low Resource Language. arXiv:2103.11596
- Licence: free for non-commercial research use
- Contents: Kr_1 stories/novels/social media, Kr_2 Hindi–Kangri dictionary, Kr_3 poems/folk songs, Kr_4 Hindi–Kangri parallel (written on assigned topics: Hospital, Media, School, Education, …)
- Known issues: 1,417 Kr_4 "Kangri" lines are copies of the Hindi; Kr_4 misaligned around lines 500–2000 and 25,500–end; 9,773 Kr_4 Kangri lines repeated inside Kr_1
- Downloaded: 14-10-2026

## Mendeley Hindi–Kangri Parallel Corpus — `raw/mendeley_hi_kangri/`
- Source: https://data.mendeley.com/datasets/3chz6dg9d9/1 (DOI 10.17632/3chz6dg9d9.1)
- Creator: Vandana Chaudhary (2026)
- Licence: CC BY 4.0
- Contents: 25,000 Hindi–Kangri pairs; domains: agriculture, plants, home remedies, food, fairs, festivals, temples, conversation
- Known issues: blank lines at Hindi 14,215 and Kangri 14,446 shift ~230 pairs by one line (fixed by dropping blanks)
- Downloaded: 14-10-2026
